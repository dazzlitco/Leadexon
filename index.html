Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers, before you ever talk to us.

What does Leadexon do?

Leadexon helps HVAC companies follow up with unanswered inquiries and re-engage leads that may otherwise go cold — turning missed opportunities into conversations your team can act on.

Do I need to replace my existing CRM?

No. The initial goal is to work alongside your existing lead and customer-management process wherever possible.

Does this replace my sales team?

No. Leadexon is designed to support your team by helping recover opportunities and create more conversations. Your team remains responsible for handling the customer and booking the job.

How quickly can we get started?

We’ll first review your current lead flow and identify where opportunities may be getting lost. From there, we’ll recommend the simplest setup for your business.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your business and the lead-recovery setup required. Request a demo and we’ll explain the options.

Do you guarantee more booked jobs?

No. Results depend on factors including lead quality, response rates, service availability, and how your team handles opportunities. Our focus is on helping you recover and follow up with leads more consistently.
